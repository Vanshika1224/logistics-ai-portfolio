# 🚚 Project: AI-Assisted Logistics Customer Service

### 🎯 Business Problem

Communicating a delayed shipment accurately, professionally, and clearly to both a customer and an internal coordination team.

### 🛠️ Tools Used

- 🤖 ChatGPT
- ✍️ Prompt engineering techniques
- 📊 Structured output formatting

### 🧠 Strategies Practised

- 🔹 Zero-shot prompting
- 🔹 Context and constraints prompting
- 🔹 Role prompting
- 🔹 Few-shot prompting
- 🔹 Structured output prompting
- 🔹 Audience-based prompting

### 📦 Outputs Created

- 📧 Customer email explaining the shipment delay
- ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) responses
- 📋 Customer support table
- 📱 SMS notification about the delay
- 📝 Internal briefing for the coordination team

### 🙋 My Contribution

I modified and improved prompts to make AI-generated responses clearer, more accurate, and suitable for different audiences. I added relevant context and constraints, specified roles and output formats, and adjusted the tone for customers and internal teams. I also checked the responses for consistency, professional language, unsupported delivery promises, and conflicting information across communication channels. ✅

### 💡 Key Learning

My most useful learning was that **better prompts lead to better AI-generated results**. I learned how context, clear instructions, constraints, and audience details improve the quality of responses. I also understood the importance of maintaining consistent information while adapting the tone and format for different audiences. 🌟

### ⚠️ Limitations

This project is a classroom simulation based on fictional information. It is not connected to live shipment tracking systems, logistics databases, or real-time delivery updates. All generated information would need to be verified before being used in a real business environment. 🔍
